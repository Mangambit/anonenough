00 / How the audit works
STEP 01

You declare what an outsider already knows

Use the column chips on the sheet above. Anything you mark is treated as something a classmate, parent or teacher could recognize on sight. Nothing else is assumed.

STEP 02

We count how many people share each answer

Every row is grouped with the rows that look identical to that outsider. The size of the smallest group is k. When k is 1, a single row belongs to one identifiable person.

STEP 03

We find the cheapest way to raise k

Every combination of coarsening is tested, and any that would destroy a table you plan to publish is thrown out. What survives is ranked by how much of your statistics it keeps.

Change any chip and all three steps rerun instantly, so you can see how much your risk depends on what you assume the outsider knows. That assumption, not the maths, is the part worth arguing about.
